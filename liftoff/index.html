Mission '36 · The ride up

Ninety seconds to orbit.

Before you stand on the Moon, you ride up, as Seeker 123. Strapped in on the crew deck, look around the cabin through ignition, max Q, the booster falling away and the sun rising early over the Gulf. When the engines cut off, unbuckle and float. The real flight, compressed about six to one.
